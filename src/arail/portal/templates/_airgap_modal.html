Known gaps — what isn't wrapped

    The Python-level guard catches requests, urllib.request, httpx and its successor httpx2 (so also the Hugging Face downloader and the Anthropic/OpenAI SDKs built on them). Other clients (aiohttp, urllib3 or httpcore used directly, raw sockets, subprocess curl) are not wrapped, and a proxy you configure is not checked — see docs/PRIVACY.md for the full list. The threat model is well-meaning agent code, not an adversary on this host.
