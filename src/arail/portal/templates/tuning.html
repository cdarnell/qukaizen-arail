Architecture — MLX unified-memory streaming

Layers live on NVMe. A rotating window of the current layer is mmap'd into unified memory; MLX kernels read it in place (no PCIe copy — that stage simply doesn't exist on Apple silicon). KV-cache can quantize mid-stream, and the prompt cache reuses prefixes across turns. These are the levers our knobs reach.
