… Work schedule

The lab paces itself by time of day. During light work hours agents stay out of your way: background deep-model runs and batch experiments are held so foreground chat stays snappy. In the heavy window the agents open the throttle — background QueueLLM inference, aggressive autoresearch, bigger batches.
